## Decisions

- **Macroeconomics (85):** Selected; expand beyond one indicator after demand validation
- **Daily reference FX (83):** Selected; comply with ECB reuse disclosures
- **Sovereign yield curves (83):** Selected; narrow US curve scope; rights review pending
- **Company fundamentals (81):** Next-stage candidate; XBRL reconciliation and history are costly
- **Derived analytics (68):** Bundle feature after input-rights and calculation validation
- **Monthly commodities (67):** Selective series rights clearance required
- **ETF holdings (65):** Lagged filings; IDs and fund mapping raise effort
- **Global equity market data (60):** No-go for resale until exchange/vendor agreements
- **Cryptocurrency (58):** No-go for public venue redistribution without permission
- **Commercial indices (50):** No-go without index licences

The top three scores are close: a one-point licensing change shifts a score by four points. Treat the shortlist as a feasibility choice and validate customer demand before final investment.
